YEBOX / STRATEGY02 / 20
02 / Strategic architecture

Shared method · Different market logic

One operating rhythm.
Three product strategies.

What is shared

  • Week 1: Positioning, audience, tone, campaign concept and message hierarchy.
  • Every month: Editorial calendar, content production, paid distribution and performance review.
  • Every product: Dedicated sales landing page and conversion journey.
  • Every campaign: Track qualified leads, onboarding and actual product use.

What stays distinct

Product-specific audiences, visual worlds, content examples, proof points, conversion actions and adoption metrics.

The governing principle

Reach is a signal.
Adoption is the outcome.

Impressions, views and engagement help diagnose distribution. They do not prove that a community is active, a business has connected its inbox or a merchant is generating repeat transactions.

Campaign sequence

run the system.

Month 03 · Optimisation

Convert & improve

  • Prioritise strongest audience and creative signals.
  • Retarget engaged prospects where tracking allows.
  • Improve landing-page conversion.
  • Follow up on onboarding and early usage.
  • Report adoption, pipeline and next-quarter needs.

AXEON / MARKET & ADOPTION13 / 20
13 / Two-sided market

Two audiences, one exchange

Merchant value creates
consumer value.

Side A · Supply

Merchants & brands

Retailers, FMCGs and lifestyle brands seeking to attract customers, deploy offers and encourage repeat purchases.

Merchant questions

  • How do offers bring in incremental visits?
  • Can promotions encourage customers to return?
  • How do we make discounting more targeted?
  • How do we measure redemption and repeat behaviour?

Acquisition route: merchant outreach, category-specific use cases, commercial demos and onboarding.

Side B · Demand

Consumers & shoppers

People who want to discover relevant offers and redeem them with participating merchants.

Consumer questions

  • What offers are available near me or for me?
  • How do I discover a participating merchant?
  • How does redemption work?
  • Is the offer worth acting on now?

Adoption route: discover offers, understand redemption, visit merchant and complete a transaction.

Strategic dependency: merchant acquisition and consumer adoption must reinforce each other. Consumer promotion without enough useful offers creates a weak experience. Merchant acquisition without customer activity weakens the commercial case.
